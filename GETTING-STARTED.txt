ZOJO LAUNCHER - getting started
===============================

1. Copy ZOJO-Launcher.apk to your phone and tap it.
   Android will ask you to allow installs from that app (Files, Chrome...). Allow it once.

2. Press the Home button. Android asks which home app to use: pick ZOJO Launcher, then "Always".
   (Or: Settings > Apps > Default apps > Home app > ZOJO Launcher.)

Using it
--------
- 01 NOW     Tap the clock for alarms. Tap a day chip for your calendar.
- 02 I/O     Battery and storage meters (tap for details). Drag the VOLUME knob up/down for media volume.
- 03 RIG     Up to 8 favourite apps as pedals. Hold a pedal to move it, remove it, or uninstall the app.
- ALL APPS   Swipe up anywhere on the home screen, or tap ALL APPS / SEARCH.
             In the list, hold any app to add it to your rig. Press Enter in search to open the top match.
- Home       Pressing Home always brings you back and closes the app list.

To go back to your old launcher: Settings > Apps > Default apps > Home app.
